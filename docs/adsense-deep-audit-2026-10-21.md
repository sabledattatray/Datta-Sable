# AdSense policy audit — 21 October 2026

## Decision

Technical corrections are implemented across dattasable.com, fabric.dattasable.com, and excel.dattasable.com. This is a readiness audit, not an approval guarantee or a certificate of legal compliance. Two owner-dependent areas remain unverified: permission to publish historical material and historical traffic sources. The owner answered that they do not remember; that answer is not treated as permission or proof of a violation.

## Scope and evidence

- Live navigation: 280 seeded public URLs and 357 distinct destinations checked. Four linked mentorship destinations returned 404 and were corrected in source. Six intentionally retired preview/recruitment routes return 404 with no discovered incoming links; they should stay retired.
- Public artifacts: 339 image/document/archive assets inventoried with hashes. All 31 public PDF/workbook downloads returned HTTP 200 and an appropriate PDF or ZIP-format signature. Private upload storage is excluded. File signatures establish delivery, not ownership, formula correctness, or absence of malware.
- Advertising: no Google advertising script was found in the 357 checked initial-HTML responses. The source defaults advertising off and limits the enabled path to published blog posts. Neither subdomain currently loads ad tags. No ads were clicked or impressions deliberately generated.
- Prior release: 68 subdomain rendered-route checks and 26 main-site metadata/crawler checks passed. The signed-in account showed ownership verified, ads.txt Authorised, review requested/Getting ready, and Policy centre No current issues. A separate European message for dattasable.com was published with explicit owner authorization.
- This pass adds a separately labelled advertising-preferences control using Google's CMP readiness API. It does not load the advertising service, grant consent, or change analytics choices. The button appears only when the revocation function is ready.

## Rights and history review still needed

Use `docs/audits/asset-rights-inventory-2026-10-21.json` as the checklist. For each used image, workbook, screenshot, certificate or third-party logo, record its creator/source, license or permission evidence, and any required attribution. An AI-created asset also needs provenance; generation is not proof that a third-party right cannot be implicated. Do not infer an infringement merely because evidence is missing. Replace or retire specific assets whose rights cannot be established after checking source records. Publication of a certificate is not proof of an unrelated professional certification.

For traffic, inspect actual acquisition records and any purchased campaigns with the owner. Never manufacture visits, solicit clicks, or use GSC removals as a way to avoid the publisher review. No new review request was submitted during this audit.

### Analytics data minimization follow-up

The deeper source review found full query strings in first-party visit tracking and Google's default page-view URL collection. The fix strips queries/fragments, reduces referrers to origins, excludes account/private paths from our explicit tracking, and sanitizes the initial Google Analytics page location/referrer without introducing additional manual page-view events. Advertising purposes remain denied by default. GA account-side enhanced-measurement settings and previously collected history are not audited or certified by this code change. No past analytics records were deleted.
