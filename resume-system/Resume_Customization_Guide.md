# Choosing and tailoring a resume

Use **Senior MIS & Reporting Analyst** for general MIS, reporting, reporting automation, reporting lead and MIS management applications. This is a private specialist profile.

Use **Power BI & Business Intelligence Analyst** for Power BI, SQL reporting, business intelligence and dashboard roles. The project and technical sections come first; employment history remains complete. Preserve the distinction between total professional tenure and tool-specific/project experience.

Use **BFSI Risk & Collections Analytics** for portfolio, collections, delinquency, recovery, credit operations and risk reporting opportunities. Keep HDFC/Kissht portfolio responsibilities and source-supported leadership scope prominent.

## Tailoring workflow

1. Record the target job title, company, location, job description and required skills.
2. Choose the closest variant. Compare requested skills with canonical employment and project evidence.
3. Reorder supported competencies, projects and bullets. Adjust summary emphasis while keeping every claim tied to that evidence.
4. Flag an unproven requirement as a gap. Do not add Fabric production deployments, RLS, gateways, cloud engineering, certifications or additional years of Power BI experience merely because a job requests them.
5. Do not change dates, employers, qualifications, workforce scope or project status. Do not turn independent projects into employer assignments.
6. Regenerate the editable DOCX, export its PDF, run text/layout validation, inspect all pages and publish the tested copies.

`data/` is the shared factual source. `templates/variants.py` controls positioning and section choices. `styles/design_tokens.py` controls shared visual tokens. The generated Markdown resume and website employment history must be updated when canonical facts change.

Use PDF unless the application explicitly requests Word. Keep the editable DOCX master for future edits. Only the Master Professional Resume is public at `/Dattatray_Sable_Resume.pdf`. The three specialized profiles and all Word masters are managed privately at `/admin/resumes`.
